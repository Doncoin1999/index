DONCOIN White Paper

DONCOIN is a daily game on the Solana blockchain: players check in once a day and share a fixed daily issuance of DON game points, of which at most 21,000,000 will ever exist. It is purely entertainment and not a financial product of any kind. This paper describes every rule, including the Founder's allocation, exactly as the on-chain program applies them.

1. How the game works

A player needs a Solana wallet with a little SOL and a computer (Windows or Mac) with a supported browser. The game runs in the DONCOIN web app and on the DONCOIN program on Solana; nobody holds players' keys or funds.

  1. Connect a wallet (for example Phantom in Chrome).
  2. Prove you are a unique person with Civic verification. One person, one account.
  3. Sign the Terms of Use with the wallet and pay a refundable account deposit (about 0.0098 SOL).
  4. Check in once a day. Each check-in earns points for that day.
  5. Earn a share of the day's issuance. After the day ends, your share is settled and locked.
  6. Wait 4 months. Each month's earnings unlock four months after that month.
  7. Convert unlocked earnings into DON in your wallet, where they can be held, sent or traded on decentralized exchanges.

Every rule in this paper is enforced by the on-chain program. Where the app and the program differ, the program decides.

2. Token and supply

At most 21,000,000 DON can ever exist. Half is issued in the first 548 days (about 18 months), and the daily amount halves every 548 days after that.

  • Name and ticker: DONCOIN, DON. A standard SPL token on Solana with 9 decimals.
  • Who can create DON: only the DONCOIN program, through a program-controlled mint authority. No person holds it. The token has no freeze authority, so nobody can freeze holders' DON.
  • No pre-mine, no sale: no DON exists at launch, and none is sold by the Founder or anyone else. Every DON is issued day by day under the rules below.
  • Token address: Published here before launch. Any token with another address is not DONCOIN, whatever its name or logo.
  • Day and month: a day is 24 hours from the launch time; a month is 30.4375 days.

3. Daily rewards

Each day's issuance is shared by points among everyone who checked in that day, plus the Founder, who always counts as one participant (section 5). No player can receive more than 1% of a day's issuance.

  • Points: one check-in per day gives 1 point. Referral bonus points (section 4) are added to the player's next check-in day.
  • Your share: day's issuance × your points ÷ (all players' points + 1 for the Founder), capped at 1% of the day's issuance (191.61 DON per day in period 1).
  • When you know the amount: only after the day ends, because it depends on how many others check in. The app shows an estimate during the day.
  • Settlement: a finished day must be settled within 30 days. The app does this automatically with the next check-in. A day not settled in time is lost to the player and goes to the remainder (section 5).
  • Lock: settled earnings go into a batch for the month they were earned and unlock four months later. Example: everything earned in month 3 can be converted from the start of month 7.

4. Invitations

A player can invite others with a single-use code; when the new player reaches 30 active days, both get 10 bonus points. There is one level only: nobody earns from the people their invitees invite.

  • Codes: created in the app, valid for 14 days, used once. Only a fingerprint (SHA-256) of the code is stored on-chain. The player shares the code themselves; the app sends no messages.
  • Deposit: about 0.0051 SOL per code, returned when the code is closed.
  • Bonus: +10 points to each, added to their next check-in day, once the invitee has 30 active days.
  • Limits: an inviter can receive at most 3 bonuses per month; pending bonus points are capped at 100; at most 10 open codes per player in the app; nobody can invite themselves.

5. Founder allocation

The Founder receives every day's remainder: whatever part of the day's issuance the players do not receive. 80% of it goes to the Founder and 20% to the Liquidity Vault. With few players this is most of each day's issuance; from about 100 players it equals one player's share.

The rules, as the program applies them

  1. One fixed wallet. The Founder's wallet address is set when the program is launched and can never be changed. The wallet is created outside the program; its address and balance are public on Solana.
  2. No activity requirement. The Founder does not check in and needs no Civic verification. The program refuses check-ins from the Founder wallet.
  3. Counts as one participant. Every day the Founder counts as 1 point when players' shares are calculated, which lowers each player's share slightly.
  4. No cap. The 1% per-person cap does not apply to the Founder.
  5. The remainder. For each day: remainder = day's issuance − everything settled to players for that day. It includes amounts cut by the 1% cap and any player shares not settled within 30 days.
  6. When it is settled. A day's remainder is settled only after its 30-day settlement window closes, one day at a time in order. Anyone can trigger this; old day records cannot be closed until it is done.
  7. The 80/20 split. 80% is credited to the Founder's account and locked for 4 months like all player earnings. 20% is issued at once as DON to the Liquidity Vault (section 6), with no lock.
  8. No fees. The Founder pays no conversion fee in DON or SOL.
  9. Same Terms. The Founder must sign the Terms of Use every 6 months like every player, and cannot convert without a valid signature.
  10. Account cannot be closed. The Founder account stays open so that remainders can always be settled.
  11. No other allocation. The Founder receives nothing else: no pre-mine, no team tokens, no sale proceeds, no share of fees.

6. Liquidity Vault and pool

The Liquidity Vault is a program account with no private key. It collects fees and the 20% remainder share, and its only possible use is to add DON and SOL in pairs to one trading pool on Raydium, where its position is locked forever.

  • What goes in: players' conversion fees (3% of converted DON plus 0.003 SOL per conversion) and 20% of every Founder remainder.
  • What can come out: nothing, except paired deposits into the pool. The Vault cannot sell DON, pay anyone, or withdraw its pool position. Nobody, including the Founder, can move its funds.
  • When the pool opens: at the earliest in month 4 (from about 21 March 2027), when the first player earnings unlock, and only once the Vault has at least 10 SOL available on top of a 1 SOL reserve for Raydium's costs.
  • Opening price: the pool opens at a start price set when the program is launched [START PRICE, SOL PER DON]. The Vault uses its available SOL and only as much DON as matches that price; the rest waits.
  • Top-ups: afterwards anyone can trigger a top-up. The Vault adds DON and SOL in the pool's current ratio, at most 20% of the pool at a time.
  • Manipulation guard: the pool price is recorded at most once an hour into a moving average. Top-ups start after 24 recordings and are refused if the price is more than 3% from the average.
  • If someone opens the pool first: the Vault adopts that pool (same fee tier) and starts adding after 24 price recordings.

A pool does not guarantee any price or that DON can be sold. Prices are set by trading between other people.

8. What can and cannot change

The supply, issuance schedule, lock period, Founder rules and Vault rules are fixed in the program. An administrator can adjust a few parameters within hard limits until the program is frozen; after that nothing can be changed. "Never" below holds fully once the right to upgrade the program has been removed; until then an upgrade could change any rule.

Program upgrades: from launch on 20 November 2026 until the freeze, the Founder holds both the administrator right and the right to upgrade the program, in a dedicated wallet, separate from the Founder's allocation wallet. The settings are frozen and the upgrade right is removed for good, making the program immutable, on 21 May 2027 or later, once all of the following have worked on mainnet: the Liquidity Vault has opened the pool and added liquidity, players have converted unlocked earnings, and at least one referral bonus has been paid. Until then, any fault can still be repaired with a program upgrade. Both steps are public on-chain transactions that anyone can verify.

DONCOIN is purely a game for entertainment, not a financial product of any kind. Every player, including the Founder, must sign the Terms of Use with their wallet before playing and again every 6 months; this White Paper forms part of those Terms.

  • Signing: the signature stores a fingerprint (SHA-256) of the exact Terms text and the date on-chain. The program refuses check-ins, invitations and conversions without a valid signature, also for anyone who bypasses the app.

  • Status: the program has been tested on Solana's devnet test network. An independent security audit is planned after launch and before the pool can open in month 4; until the audit is published, treat the software as unaudited. Numbers in this paper follow the program's current code.
