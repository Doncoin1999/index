How it works

One minute a day

You need a computer (Windows or Mac), a Solana wallet such as Phantom, and a little SOL for network fees. Nobody holds your keys or your funds.

Connect your wallet

Connect a Solana wallet such as Phantom. One account per computer: the app links your computer to the wallet you register with.

Sign the Terms

Sign the Terms of Use with your wallet and pay a refundable account deposit of about 0.0098 SOL.

Check in daily

Each check-in earns a point. When the day ends, the day's DON is shared by points among everyone who played.

Unlock and convert

Each month's earnings unlock after 4 months. Then convert them to DON in your wallet, to hold or send.

Tokenomics

Bitcoin's supply design, on a faster clock

Bitcoin showed that a hard cap and a halving schedule nobody can change make supply fully predictable. DONCOIN uses the same cap and the same halving shape. The difference is the clock: DON halves every 18 months instead of every four years, and it is earned by playing, not by mining.

Share of maximum supply issued

Both reach 50%, 75%, 87.5% … at each halving. DON starts at 3.1% (the Development Team allocation) and gets there about 2.7 times faster.

Built by playing

A base made of people, not buyers

No DON is ever sold. Apart from the Development Team's 650,000 DON (3.1%), every coin is issued to people who show up, one day at a time. The earlier and more steadily people play, the larger their part of the supply, and the more widely DON ends up spread.

  • One account per computerThe app allows only one new account per computer, and none from private or incognito windows. Not perfect, but it keeps the game fair for everyday players.
  • Fair daily sharesNo account can take more than 1% of a day's issuance, so DON spreads as the player base grows.
  • Invite friends, both winWhen someone you invite reaches 30 active days, you each get 10 bonus points. One level only, no pyramids.
  • Fees build the marketHalf of every DON conversion fee and all SOL fees go to a keyless Liquidity Vault whose only use is adding liquidity to a DON/SOL pool.

Full Founder rules in the White Paper

The Liquidity Vault

A program account with no private key. It collects half of the DON conversion fee (1.5% of converted DON) plus 0.003 SOL per conversion, and 20% of every Founder remainder.

  • Its only possible use is adding DON and SOL in pairs to one Raydium pool
  • The pool can open from month 4 (about 21 March 2027) once the Vault holds 10 SOL
  • Its pool position is locked forever; nobody, including the Founder, can withdraw it
  • Top-ups are guarded by an hourly price average against manipulation

A pool does not guarantee any price or that DON can be sold.

The Development Team allocation

The Development Team, which builds and runs the game, receives 650,000 DON (about 3.1% of the maximum supply) at launch, to its own wallet fixed in the program.

  • Credited in advance and taken from the start of the issuance schedule, so the total stays within 21,000,000: the first halving comes 34 days earlier, on day 514 (17 April 2028) instead of day 548
  • Locked for 4 months like all earnings, so it unlocks in month 4 (about 21 March 2027)
  • No check-ins, normal conversion fees (3%, split equally between the Liquidity Vault and the Founder), and nothing else from the game

Read before you play

This is a game. It is not an investment.

Play DONCOIN only for fun, and only with money you are happy to spend on a game. DON tokens are game points. Do not buy, hold or play DON expecting to make money.

No value is promised

DON may never have any value, price or market. Bitcoin's supply design says nothing about what DON will be worth.

You will pay costs

Network fees, conversion fees and deposits are part of playing. Expect to lose all of them; deposits are returned only if the program works as intended.

Unaudited at launch

The program has been tested on Solana's devnet. An independent audit is planned before month 4. Until it is published, treat the software as unaudited.

Founder and team allocations

The Development Team gets 650,000 DON at launch, and with few daily players most of each day's DON goes to the Founder and the Vault. Read these rules before you play.

Technical and wallet risk

Software errors, hacks, lost keys and failures of Solana or Raydium can all cause losses. Nobody can recover a lost wallet. The one-account-per-computer check can be bypassed by determined players.

Fakes, law and tax

Only the official token address is DONCOIN. Laws may change; checking whether you may play and what tax applies is your responsibility.
