2. Playing costs money, and you will not get it back

Playing the game has costs, including Solana network fees, conversion fees paid in SOL and DONCOIN, and deposits for game accounts. You accept that you may lose all such costs, and you do not expect to get any of them back in any way. Any amount the game program returns, such as a deposit when an account is closed, is not guaranteed.

3. You accept all risks and make no claims

4. The Founder's allocation

You understand and accept that the founder of DONCOIN (the Founder) is treated differently from players, as follows:

  1. The Founder's wallet is fixed when the game program is launched and can never be changed.
  2. The Founder has no activity requirement: the Founder does not check in and needs no identity verification.
  3. Every day the Founder counts as one participant when players' shares are calculated, which lowers each player's share.
  4. The per-player cap (1% of each day's issuance) does not apply to the Founder.
  5. Every day the Founder receives the remainder: the day's issuance minus everything settled to players for that day, including amounts cut by the per-player cap and player shares not settled within 30 days. With few players this can be most of each day's issuance.
  6. 80% of the remainder goes to the Founder, locked for 4 months like players' earnings. 20% goes to the Liquidity Vault at once.
  7. The Founder pays no conversion fees.
  8. The Founder must sign these Terms every 6 months like every player.
  9. The Founder receives no other allocation: no pre-mine, no sale proceeds and no share of fees.

5. The Development Team's allocation

You understand and accept that the Development Team, whose wallet is fixed when the game program is launched and can never be changed, receives 650,000 DONCOIN (about 3.1% of the maximum supply) at launch, before any player has played. This allocation is credited in advance and taken from the start of the issuance schedule, so the first halving comes 34 days earlier (on day 514 instead of day 548). It is locked for 4 months like players' earnings. The Development Team does not check in, receives nothing else from the game and pays the normal conversion fees.

6. Game rules and the White Paper

The DONCOIN White Paper describes all rules of the game, including supply, issuance, rewards, invitations, the Founder's and the Development Team's allocations, the Liquidity Vault and fees. The version published when you sign forms part of these Terms. Where the White Paper and the game program differ, the program decides.

7. One account per computer

You may have only one DONCOIN game account per computer. To limit this, the DONCOIN app stores a random code on your computer, in your browser's storage and a cookie, linked to the wallet you register with. The code stays on your computer and is not sent to DONCOIN or anyone else. By signing you consent to this storage. You may delete it, but you must not create more than one account per computer.
